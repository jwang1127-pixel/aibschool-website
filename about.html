ABOUT AI B SCHOOL

我们为什么存在
Why We Exist

AI B School 是一个面向 AI 时代的能力标准、训练与战略设计平台。我们关注的不是“会不会使用某个工具”，而是人在 AI 协作环境中如何继续创造价值。

AI B School is a platform for capability standards, training, and strategic design in the AI era. Our central question is not whether people can use a particular tool, but how they continue to create value while working with AI.

OUR QUESTION · 核心问题

当机器越来越聪明，人还凭什么创造价值？

我们的回答 · Our Answer

人类的长期价值不在于与机器比计算，而在于愿景、判断、创造、责任与协作。AI 应当放大这些能力，而不是替代人的主体性。

Human value does not come from competing with machines at computation. It comes from vision, judgment, creativity, responsibility, and collaboration. AI should amplify these capabilities rather than displace human agency.

我们的实践 · Our Practice

我们通过真实项目、能力档案、过程证据和独立验证，将抽象能力变成可观察、可训练、可讨论的现实成果。

Through real projects, capability dossiers, process evidence, and independent review, we turn abstract capabilities into observable, trainable, and discussable outcomes.
