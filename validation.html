VALIDATION STANDARD

作品即凭证，
但凭证必须经得起独立检验。

Work is the credential,
but the credential must withstand independent review.

AI B School 的验证体系，不以自我评价代替证据，不以推广关系代替独立判断。

AI B School does not substitute self-assessment for evidence, or promotional relationships for independent judgment.

WHY VALIDATION MATTERS

AI 让“看起来很好”变得廉价

AI makes “looking impressive” inexpensive

文字、图像、代码和商业计划都可以快速生成。因此，真正稀缺的不是漂亮输出，而是可追溯的过程、清晰的判断、真实的责任与可验证的结果。

Text, images, code, and business plans can be generated rapidly. What remains scarce is a traceable process, clear judgment, real responsibility, and verifiable outcomes.

THE FOUR-LAYER STANDARD

四层证据结构

A four-layer evidence structure

1. Real Project

项目必须面对真实用户、真实约束或真实公共问题，而不是只为展示而设计。

The project must address real users, constraints, or public problems—not exist only for display.

2. Process Trace

保留关键决定、失败、反馈、迭代与 AI 使用过程。

Preserve key decisions, failures, feedback, iterations, and AI-use records.

3. Capability Dossier

将愿景、判断、创造与 Agency 的证据整理成结构化档案。

Organize evidence of vision, judgment, creativity, and agency into a structured dossier.

4. Independent Review

由与结果无利益关系的第三方进行审阅，并明确审阅范围。

Use third-party reviewers with no stake in the outcome and a clearly defined review scope.

THE FIREWALL

利益隔离，是公信力的底线

Independence is the foundation of credibility

推广不验证

Promoters do not validate

参与招生、推广或转介的人，不进入评审池。

Anyone involved in recruitment, promotion, or referral is excluded from review.

导师不自证

Mentors do not self-certify

指导项目的人不能作为唯一验证者。

The project mentor cannot serve as the sole validator.

证据可追溯

Evidence remains traceable

结论必须能够回到具体材料、记录和作品。

Every conclusion must trace back to specific records, artifacts, and work.
