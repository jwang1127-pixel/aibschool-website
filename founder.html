我越来越相信，AI 时代真正需要重构的，不是工具清单，而是人的能力结构。愿景力决定做什么，判断力决定什么值得相信，创造力把它变成现实，Agency 则把三者统一为人的行动。
I have come to believe that what must be redesigned in the AI era is not a list of tools, but the structure of human capability. Vision determines what to pursue, judgment determines what to trust, creativity turns it into reality, and agency unifies all three in action.
