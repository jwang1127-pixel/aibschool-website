In the AI era,
capability must be visible,
and credentials must be verifiable.

Work is the credential.

When AI can write, calculate, and answer on your behalf, adjectives on a résumé lose value. What endures is the real work you create—and whether it can withstand independent review.

OUR POSITION · 我们的立场OUR POSITION

我们不是又一个培训班。
我们在定义一套能力标准。

We are not another tutoring program.
We are defining a capability standard.

旧的凭证正在失效

Old credentials are losing signal value

分数、证书、活动清单，以及包装出来的“领导力”，在 AI 可以批量生成完美文书的时代，甄别价值正在下降。

Scores, certificates, activity lists, and polished narratives carry less signal when AI can mass-produce persuasive applications.

新的凭证必须可验证

New credentials must be verifiable

真实的作品，加上独立的验证。一个真实运行的项目、一份完整的能力档案，以及一次利益无关的第三方审阅。

Real work plus independent validation. A functioning project, a complete capability dossier, and review by a disinterested third party.

THE KEYSTONE · 拱心石THE KEYSTONE

自己说好，不算数。

Self-assertion is not validation.

能力凭证的价值，取决于验证者是否与结果保持利益独立。

The value of a capability credential depends on the reviewer’s independence from the outcome.

Capability Dossier

记录问题定义、关键判断、人机贡献、迭代过程和最终成果。

Documents problem definition, key judgments, human–AI contribution, iteration, and results.

Independent Review

由不了解学生、与项目无商业利益关系的专家进行审阅。

Reviewed by experts who do not know the student and have no commercial stake in the project.

Evidence Firewall

推广、转介与验证严格隔离，保护标准的公信力。

Promotion, referral, and validation remain separated to protect credibility.
